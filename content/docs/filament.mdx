## Before you print

- Check the table for your material **and** colour before you slice — the machines have verified profiles per material (see each [machine page](/docs/machines)).
- Make sure the stock column shows enough rolls for your whole print.

## Low-stock alerts

Rows highlighted in amber (2 rolls or fewer, marked "· low") are running out. If the filament you need is low — or gone — use the **Request filament** button above the table: it sends a restock request straight to exco's ops dashboard, and the inventory updates once it's fulfilled.
