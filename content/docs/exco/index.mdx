Everything in this section is **admin-only**: the pages carry `admin: true` in
their frontmatter, so they appear in the sidebar, search and exports only when
you are signed in at [`/edit`](/edit). Members never see them — not even as
403s; to them these pages simply do not exist.

This is the shelf for internal runbooks. Current pages:

Adding a new runbook: create the file in this folder from the
[writing guide](/docs/exco/writing-pages), start its frontmatter with
`admin: true`, and list it in this folder's `meta.json`.
