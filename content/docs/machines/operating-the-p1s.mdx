This is the SOP for the three P1S printers — [Left](/docs/machines/p1s-left), [Middle](/docs/machines/p1s-middle) and [Right](/docs/machines/p1s-right). Read it once before your first print: what you can print, how to send a job from Bambu Studio, what to watch for, and what to do when a print goes wrong.

## What you can print

| Material | Who can use it |
| --- | --- |
| Polymaker CoPE | Anyone |
| Polymaker PETG | Anyone |
| Anything else | Ask an Exco or Helper first — a quick message is enough |

<Callout type="warn" title="Staff handle the AMS">
  Don't load or unload filament yourself. Helpers, Lab Assistants and Exco are the only ones who touch the AMS.
</Callout>

## Sending a print from Bambu Studio

### 1. Pick the printer

<Steps>
  <Step>Work on the lab PC — Bambu Studio is already installed.</Step>
  <Step>Open it and go to the **Device** page.</Step>
  <Step>Make sure the right printer (Bambu Lab P1S) is selected.</Step>
</Steps>

![Bambu Studio's Device page — make sure the correct P1S is selected.](/p1s-sop/01-device-page.webp)

### 2. Set up the print

<Steps>
  <Step>Click **Prepare** and drop in your model.</Step>
  <Step>Rotate it into a sensible orientation.</Step>
  <Step>Hit **Sync Info** — that pulls in machine and filament data.</Step>
  <Step>Pick the right filament and apply it to the model.</Step>
  <Step>
    Choose the preset:

    - CoPE: **0.2mm CoPE 200 - General**
    - PETG: no preset yet — ask a Helper or Exco what to use
  </Step>
</Steps>

![The Prepare tab — insert the model, sync info, pick the filament and the preset.](/p1s-sop/02-configure-print.webp)

### 3. Supports and brim

Turn both of these on, basically always:

- **Supports** — for anything with overhangs
- **Brim** — keeps the print stuck to the bed

![Process settings — supports and brim should always be turned on.](/p1s-sop/03-supports-brim.webp)

### 4. Before you slice

Thirty seconds of checking beats a failed four-hour print. Confirm the filament is right, the preset is applied, and supports and brim are on.

### 5. Slice and send

<Steps>
  <Step>Click **Slice**.</Step>
  <Step>Look over the time and material estimates on the preview.</Step>
  <Step>Happy with it? Hit **Print** and the job goes to the machine.</Step>
</Steps>

![Slice the plate, review the time and material estimates, then print.](/p1s-sop/04-slice-print.webp)

## Watch the first layers

<Callout type="warn">Stay for the first 1–3 layers. Not optional.</Callout>

If the first layer goes down smooth and sticks, you're free to go. Spaghetti, lifting or shifting instead? Cancel it, work out what went wrong, then retry.

## While it runs

You can leave the lab while your print runs — nobody expects you to watch the camera. Lab Assistants keep an eye on running prints and flag problems in the WhatsApp group.

## Prints longer than 5 hours

<Callout type="warn">Don't start these during the day.</Callout>

Talk to a Helper or Exco first and they'll set you up with a nighttime slot.

## When it's done

### 1. Pop it off

<Steps>
  <Step>Give the bed a moment to cool.</Step>
  <Step>Work the print loose with the plastic scraper.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Two golden rules">
  Plastic scraper only — never metal tools — and go easy on the plate. A scratched
  build plate prints badly for everyone after you.
</Callout>

### 2. Put the plate back

Straight back onto the magnetic bed, not the bench. This is the single most forgotten step.

### 3. Clean it (optional, but worth it)

Dish soap and water, or diluted IPA.

## When a print fails

1. Report it — right away if you're standing there; if not, a Lab Assistant will report it for you.
2. Printer seems fine otherwise? Just reprint.
3. Real trouble — clog, mechanical weirdness: don't try to fix it yourself. Get a Technician or Exco.

## Switching materials

No purging routine between CoPE and PETG. Just ask a Helper or Lab Assistant to swap the AMS filament.

## Special materials

If you've got approval for something other than CoPE or PETG, know your temperatures and profile before you start. Unsure? Ask. Don't guess.

## End of day

Nothing to shut down. The printers run 24/7, and quiet nights with an empty queue are when calibration gets done.

## Quick do's and don'ts

| # | Do | Don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Print CoPE or PETG freely (anything else: ask first) | Touch the AMS filament yourself |
| 2 | Watch the first 1–3 layers | Use metal tools on the plate |
| 3 | Put the build plate back right away | Scrape the plate hard |
| 4 | Arrange >5h prints with Exco/Helper in advance | Start long prints during the day |
| 5 | Ask when unsure | Guess settings for special materials |
