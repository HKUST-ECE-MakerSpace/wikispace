The standard operating procedure for the three P1S printers — [Left](/docs/machines/p1s-left), [Middle](/docs/machines/p1s-middle) and [Right](/docs/machines/p1s-right). It covers what you may print, how to set up and send a job from Bambu Studio, what to watch while it runs, and what to do when it's done (or fails).

## Material Usage Rules

| Material | Authorization |
| --- | --- |
| Polymaker CoPE | Free to use (any member) |
| Polymaker PETG | Free to use (any member) |
| Any other material | Requires prior approval from ANY Exco or Helper (verbal/text/any format) |

<Callout type="warn" title="AMS filament is handled by staff only">
  Members cannot change or refill filament into the AMS. Only Helpers, Lab Assistants, and Exco may handle filament loading/unloading.
</Callout>

## Pre-Print Setup

### Step 1: Prepare Your File

<Steps>
  <Step>Use the dedicated lab PC with Bambu Studio installed.</Step>
  <Step>Open Bambu Studio and go to the **Device** page.</Step>
  <Step>Ensure the correct printer (Bambu Lab P1S) is selected.</Step>
</Steps>

![Bambu Studio's Device page — make sure the correct P1S is selected.](/p1s-sop/01-device-page.webp)

### Step 2: Configure Your Print

<Steps>
  <Step>Click **Prepare** and insert your 3D model file.</Step>
  <Step>Adjust orientation as needed (use the rotation tools).</Step>
  <Step>Click **Sync Info** — it syncs both machine and filament data.</Step>
  <Step>Choose the correct filament and apply it to your model.</Step>
  <Step>
    Select the correct printing preset:

    - For CoPE: use **0.2mm CoPE 200 - General**.
    - For PETG: preset not yet created — check with a Helper/Exco for guidance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![The Prepare tab — insert the model, sync info, pick the filament and the preset.](/p1s-sop/02-configure-print.webp)

### Step 3: Support & Adhesion

Always turn ON:

- **Supports** — enable as needed for overhangs.
- **Brim** — for better bed adhesion.

![Process settings — supports and brim should always be turned on.](/p1s-sop/03-supports-brim.webp)

### Step 4: Final Check

Double-check:

- Correct filament selected.
- Correct preset applied.
- Supports and brim ON.

### Step 5: Slice & Print

<Steps>
  <Step>Click **Slice** to generate the G-code.</Step>
  <Step>Review the estimated print time and material usage on the preview screen.</Step>
  <Step>If everything looks correct, click **Print** to send the job to the printer.</Step>
</Steps>

![Slice the plate, review the time and material estimates, then print.](/p1s-sop/04-slice-print.webp)

## First Layer Monitoring

<Callout type="warn">Mandatory: watch the first 1–3 layers of your print.</Callout>

- If the first layer looks good (smooth, sticking properly), you may leave.
- If it fails (spaghetti, not sticking, shifting), cancel immediately and re-evaluate.

## During the Print

- You are allowed to leave the lab while the print runs.
- You do not need to monitor the camera remotely.
- Lab Assistants will monitor prints and announce any issues in the WhatsApp group chat.

## Long Prints (>5 Hours)

<Callout type="warn">Prints longer than 5 hours should not be started during daytime.</Callout>

Contact a Helper/Exco before starting — they will arrange a nighttime print slot for you.

## Post-Print Procedure

### Step 1: Remove Your Print

<Steps>
  <Step>Wait for the bed to cool slightly.</Step>
  <Step>Use the provided spatula/scraper to gently remove the print.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Two golden rules">
  **No metal tools** — use only the provided plastic scraper. **Do not scrape the build plate** —
  be gentle to avoid damaging the surface.
</Callout>

### Step 2: Return the Build Plate

Immediately place the build plate back onto the magnetic bed after removing your print.

### Step 3: Clean the Plate (Optional but Recommended)

Wipe/wash the build plate with:

- Dish soap + water, **or**
- Diluted Isopropanol (IPA)

## Print Failures

If a print fails:

1. **Report it** — if you're present, report immediately. If not, a Lab Assistant will report for you.
2. If the printer is fine: you may restart / reprint.
3. If a serious error occurs (e.g., print head clog, mechanical issue):
   - Do **not** attempt to fix it yourself.
   - Contact a Technician or Exco member immediately.

## Material Switching

- Members do not need to perform any cleaning/purging procedure when switching between CoPE and PETG.
- Simply notify a Helper/Assistant to switch the AMS filament for you.

## Special Materials

If you have approval to use a special (non-CoPE/PETG) material:

- You should clearly know the required settings (temperature, profile, etc.).
- If you don't know exactly what you're doing — ask for help. Do not guess.

## Shutdown / End of Day

- No shutdown procedure required.
- The printer may run 24/7.
- If the printer has no active jobs and it's nighttime, calibration may be performed.

## General Reminders

| # | Do | Don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Use only CoPE or PETG without approval | Change/refill AMS filament yourself |
| 2 | Watch first 1–3 layers | Use metal tools on the plate |
| 3 | Put build plate back immediately | Scrape the build plate aggressively |
| 4 | Contact Exco/Helper for prints >5 hours | Start long prints during daytime without approval |
| 5 | Ask for help if unsure | Guess settings for special materials |
