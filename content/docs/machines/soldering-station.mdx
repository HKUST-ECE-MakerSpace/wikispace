## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step>Power on the station and select your target temperature.</Step>
  <Step>Wait for the tip to reach temperature (LED stops blinking).</Step>
  <Step>Tin the tip lightly with fresh solder before use.</Step>
  <Step>Solder your joint using proper technique.</Step>
  <Step>Return the iron to its stand and power off after use.</Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Tip Not Heating">
    Check power connection, verify tip is seated fully, inspect heating element cable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Poor Solder Flow / Cold Joints">
    Re-tin the tip, clean with brass wool, increase temp slightly, check flux freshness.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tip Oxidizing Quickly">
    Always tin before storing, avoid leaving tip on at idle for long periods.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Repair Guides

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Clean Soldering Iron Tip">
    **Difficulty:** Easy · **Tools:** Wet sponge or brass wool
    
    Wet the sponge with water and squeeze out excess (should be damp, not dripping). For brass wool: gently push and twist the tip into the wool to remove excess solder. For wet sponge: gently wipe the tip on the damp sponge. After cleaning, immediately apply fresh solder to the tip (tin it) to prevent oxidation.
    
    <Callout type="warn">Do not use steel wool - it will scratch and damage the tip plating. Do not use excessive force when cleaning. Always tin the tip after cleaning.</Callout>
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Rules

- Always use the wet sponge or brass wool to clean the tip.
- Do not use steel wool - it scratches and damages the tip plating.
- Never leave the iron on and unattended.
- Turn off and let cool before storing.
- Tin the tip before storing to prevent oxidation.
- Use fume extraction when available.
- Do not use excessive force when soldering or cleaning.
- Do not use temperatures higher than 400°C.
- Return the iron to its stand when not in use.
- Report faulty tips or stations to exco immediately.
