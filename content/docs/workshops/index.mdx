A workshop is a runner walking a group through one topic: a machine induction, a skill, or a build, following a structured session plan. Sessions run in the lab (RM3125A, Academic Building).

## The pathway

Everything starts with the **induction workshops** (lab access and safety, electronics bench, machine shop, and your first FDM print). After that, two doors open: **core skill workshops** you can take in any order, and **machine workshops** that each certify you on one machine.

## How workshop pages work

Every workshop page has two tabs. The **Overview** covers what the session teaches, how long it runs, who it's for, and the flow of the day. The **Checklist** tab is an interactive, tickable list of session items grouped into sections (before the session, session flow, wrap-up); progress saves per device, and there's a print view if you'd rather work from paper. Link runners straight to the checklist with `?tab=checklist` on the page URL.

The checklist, duration and audience are declared in the page's frontmatter, so the wiki builds the tabs automatically. No layout work needed.
