Workshops are how the Makerspace teaches: a runner takes a group through a topic — machine induction, a skill, a build — following a structured session plan.

## How workshop pages work

Every workshop page has two parts:

1. **An overview** — what the session covers, how long it runs, who it's for, and the flow of the day.
2. **A checklist** — an interactive, tickable list of session items grouped into sections (before the session, session flow, wrap-up). Progress is saved per device, and there's a print view for paper people.

The checklist, duration and audience are declared in the page's frontmatter, so the wiki renders the interactive checklist automatically — no layout work needed.
