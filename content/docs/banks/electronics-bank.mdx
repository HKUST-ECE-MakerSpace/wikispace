One map for the whole electronics workstation: every box is a cabinet or drawer you can walk to, labelled with its code (rows 1–13, letters A–B, then the thin C–I drawers below), and the text inside says what's in it. The unlabeled boxes under the last row are the lower bench drawers; the cabinets from 8 onwards hold the SMT towers, whose drawer codes (R/C/D/Q/U) are decoded in the value charts further down.

![How the electronics workstation looks on the bench — cabinet codes 1A to 13B. Outlined empty boxes are the 5B and 6B drawers.](/banks/electronics-workstation.webp)

## Finding things

Search the grid for a part name — `heatshrink`, `0805`, `Modules` — and matching drawers light up with their coordinates. Full screen expands the whole bank; merged boxes mirror the physical bins, one box per drawer. Empty outlined slots are the 5B and 6B drawers, currently empty.

<Callout type="info">
  Empty slot, or the part you need isn't there? Hit **Request a restock** above the grid and pick the part (or "Something else…"); it lands in exco's ops dashboard queue. No account needed.
</Callout>

## SMT Drawer Values

The SMT towers (cabinets 8–13 on the grid above) are organised by component letter and drawer code — a drawer labelled `R39 (0402)` on the map is decoded here. Resistor values follow the standard E-series: E24 ({1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.5, 1.6, 1.8, 2.0, 2.2, 2.4, 2.7, 3.0, 3.3, 3.6, 3.9, 4.3, 4.7, 5.1, 5.6, 6.2, 6.8, 7.5, 8.2, 9.1} × each decade), with E12 and E6 subsets for the coarser drawers.
