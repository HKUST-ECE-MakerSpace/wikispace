The labelled grid of storage slots for electronic components: dev boards, sensors, modules, wire and prototyping supplies. Rows are numbered and columns lettered, so a grid coordinate is a real place you can walk to.

<ComponentBank id="electronics-bank" />

## Finding things

Search the grid — type "wire" or a module name and matching drawers light up with their coordinates, so you can go straight to the right bin. The **Full screen** button expands the grid for a proper look, and merged boxes mirror the physical bins: one box, one drawer.

<Callout type="info">
  Something missing or empty? Hit **Request a restock** above the grid, pick the part (or "Something else…"), and it lands in exco's ops dashboard queue. No account needed.
</Callout>
