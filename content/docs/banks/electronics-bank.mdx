The labelled grid of storage slots for electronic components: dev boards, sensors, modules, wire and prototyping supplies. Rows are numbered and columns lettered, so a grid coordinate is a real place you can walk to.

<ComponentBank id="electronics-bank" />

## Finding things

The grid above mirrors the physical bank. Each labelled cell tells you what lives at that coordinate, and cells with extra detail list the individual items inside.

<Callout type="info">
  Something missing or empty? Submit a component request (the request button on the [filament page](/docs/filament) works as a template), or tell exco directly; it lands in the ops dashboard request queue.
</Callout>
