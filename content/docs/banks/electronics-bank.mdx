The electronics bank is the labelled grid of storage slots for electronic components: dev boards, sensors, modules, wire and prototyping supplies.

<ComponentBank id="electronics-bank" />

## Finding things

The grid above mirrors the physical bank — rows are numbered, columns are lettered, and each labelled cell tells you what lives at that coordinate. Cells with extra detail list the individual items inside.

<Callout type="info">
  Need something that's empty or missing? Submit a component request — use the request button on the [filament page](/docs/filament) as a template or tell exco directly, and it lands in the ops dashboard request queue.
</Callout>
