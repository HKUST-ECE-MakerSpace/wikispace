## Drawer contents

The same bank from the drawers' point of view — search a part name (e.g. `0805`, `Modules`, `heatshrink`) and matching drawers light up. Drawers 5B and 6B are currently empty.

<ComponentBank id="electronics-drawers" />

## Finding things

Search the grid for a part name and matching drawers light up with their coordinates. Full screen expands the whole bank; merged boxes mirror the physical bins, one box per drawer. The SMT tower codes (R/C/D/Q/U/L) are listed per drawer in [SMT Drawer Values](/docs/banks/electronics-smt).

<Callout type="info">
  Empty slot, or the part you need isn't there? Hit **Request a restock** above the grid and pick the part (or "Something else…"); it lands in exco's ops dashboard queue. No account needed.
</Callout>
