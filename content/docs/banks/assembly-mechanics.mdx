The mechanical catch-all: brackets, structural hardware, assembly kits and build supplies, everything that isn't a screw, organised in a labelled coordinate grid.

<ComponentBank id="assembly-mechanics" />

## Finding things

Search the grid for a part name and matching drawers light up with their coordinates. Full screen expands the whole bank; merged boxes mirror the physical bins, one box per drawer.

<Callout type="info">
  Empty slot, or the part you need isn't there? Hit **Request a restock** above the grid and pick the part (or "Something else…"); it lands in exco's ops dashboard queue. No account needed.
</Callout>
