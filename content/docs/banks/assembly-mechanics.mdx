The assembly & mechanics bank holds everything mechanical that isn't a screw: brackets, structural hardware, assembly kits and build supplies, organised in a labelled coordinate grid.

<ComponentBank id="assembly-mechanics" />

## Finding things

Match a physical slot's row number and column letter to the grid above to see what belongs there. Cells with extra detail list the individual items inside.

<Callout type="info">
  Need something that's empty or missing? Submit a component request — use the request button on the [filament page](/docs/filament) as a template or tell exco directly, and it lands in the ops dashboard request queue.
</Callout>
