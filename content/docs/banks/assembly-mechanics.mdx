## Finding things

Search the grid for a tool name and matching drawers light up with their coordinates. Full screen expands the whole bank; merged boxes mirror the physical bins, one box per drawer.

<Callout type="info">
  Empty slot, or the tool you need isn't there? Hit **Request a restock** above the grid and pick the item (or "Something else…"); it lands in exco's ops dashboard queue. No account needed.
</Callout>
