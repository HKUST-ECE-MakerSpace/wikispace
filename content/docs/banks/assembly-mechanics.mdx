One map for the assembly workstation: every box is a cabinet or drawer you can walk to, labelled with its code (14–18, letters A–G), and the text inside says what's in it — saws, files, drills, measuring tools, PPE and consumables.

![How the assembly workstation looks on the bench — cabinet codes 14A to 18B, including the new 16G and 17C/17D drawers.](/banks/assembly-workstation.webp)

## Finding things

Search the grid for a tool name and matching drawers light up with their coordinates. Full screen expands the whole bank; merged boxes mirror the physical bins, one box per drawer.

<Callout type="info">
  Empty slot, or the tool you need isn't there? Hit **Request a restock** above the grid and pick the item (or "Something else…"); it lands in exco's ops dashboard queue. No account needed.
</Callout>
