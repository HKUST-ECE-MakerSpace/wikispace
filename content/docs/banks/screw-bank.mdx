The screw bank is the fastener wall: M2–M6 screws in organised sizes, plus nuts, washers, standoffs, threaded inserts, springs, bearings, gears and belts.

## Finding things

Each labelled cell in the grid above maps to a physical bin — find your size on the grid, then match the label on the wall. Thicker grid lines mark shelf boundaries.
