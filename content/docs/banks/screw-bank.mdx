Everything fastener-shaped lives on the screw wall: M2–M6 screws organised by size, plus nuts, washers, standoffs, threaded inserts, springs, bearings, gears and belts. If you're mid-assembly and short a screw, this is the first place to look.

<ComponentBank id="screw-bank" />

## Finding things

Search for a size or part ("M3", "bearings") and the matching drawers light up with their coordinates. Full screen gives you the whole wall at once; merged boxes are the physical bins, and thicker lines mark shelf boundaries.

<Callout type="info">
  Running low on a size? Hit **Request a restock** above the grid and pick the part; it lands in exco's ops dashboard queue. No account needed.
</Callout>
