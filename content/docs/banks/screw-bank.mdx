Everything fastener-shaped lives on the screw wall: M2–M6 screws organised by size, plus nuts, washers, standoffs, threaded inserts, springs, bearings, gears and belts. If you're mid-assembly and short a screw, this is the first place to look.

<ComponentBank id="screw-bank" />

## Finding things

Each labelled cell in the grid maps to a physical bin. Find your size on the grid, then match the label on the wall; the thicker grid lines mark shelf boundaries.

<Callout type="info">
  Running low on a size? Submit a component request: use the request button on the [filament page](/docs/filament) as a template, or tell exco directly. Either way it lands in the ops dashboard request queue.
</Callout>
